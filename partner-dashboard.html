📚 How to Use

1. Go Online

Click "Start Booking" button to go online. Your GPS location will be captured and you'll be visible to customers.

2. Accept Trip Requests

When a customer books a ride and admin assigns you, you'll see an orange notification with trip details. Click "ACCEPT" to start the trip.

3. Pickup Customer

Drive to pickup location. Click "I HAVE ARRIVED" when you reach. Customer will be notified.

4. Complete Trip

After reaching destination, click "END TRIP". Payment will be added to your revenue.

5. Track Revenue

Click "Total Revenue" or "Today Revenue" in menu to see your earnings and trip history.

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Keep GPS/Location enabled at all times
  • Update your vehicle number if changed
  • Be polite and professional with customers
  • Contact support for payment issues
